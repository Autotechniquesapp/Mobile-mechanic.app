# Sponsor Mobile Mechanic AI

A roadside repair often starts with a phone call and a texted address. By the time a mechanic writes the invoice, the customer's original complaint, the test results and the approved price can be in three different places. AutoTechniques is building Mobile Mechanic AI to keep that information with the job.

## Two features we’re seeking sponsorship for

**Google Maps for service addresses.** Help cover API usage for looking up customer service addresses and placing them on a shop’s map. Basic directions links can already open Google Maps. This pilot focuses on address lookup inside the app.

**AI workups after customer intake.** A customer might write “it clicks once when I turn the key” under **Customer states**. We want the technician to open that intake and find suggested follow-up questions and checks, such as testing battery voltage under load and checking the cable connections. The technician verifies the cause on the vehicle. Sponsorship would cover the AI API calls needed to generate and test those workups with participating shops.

Local parts stores and commercial suppliers are welcome to support a pilot for nearby shops. If you would like a sponsor credit, we can agree on placement before the pilot begins.

A sponsor can back either feature or both, fund a one-time pilot, or contribute toward monthly API usage. We can agree on the budget and time period up front. [Discuss an API sponsorship](mailto:support@mobile-mechanic.app?subject=Mobile%20Mechanic%20AI%20API%20sponsorship).

## Other ways your support could help

- **Time in the field.** Help cover development and testing with independent shops, from the first intake link to the final invoice.
- **Reliable phone workflows.** Support work on job alerts, scheduling and estimate approvals across the phones technicians use.
- **Operating costs.** Help cover the database and storage that keep a shop’s job records available.
